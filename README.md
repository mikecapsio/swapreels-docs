# SwapReels Public Documentation

SwapReels is an AI video character swap for short-form video. Paste a public TikTok, Instagram Reel, or YouTube Short, upload a photo, and download a new clip. The moves, hook, and original audio stay. The person changes.

SwapReels works on a clip that already exists. It does not write a new scene from a text prompt, and it does not merge two videos together.

## At a glance

- Paste a public TikTok video, Instagram Reel, or YouTube Shorts link. You do not download the clip into an editor first.
- Upload a clear photo (JPG, PNG, or WebP) of the person who should appear.
- The result keeps the source motion, hook, and original audio.
- The swap replaces the person in that one clip, including full-body performances such as dances and product reveals.
- Preview the video in the browser, then download an MP4.
- Paid plans include HD downloads with no watermark.
- Source clips run from 5 to 30 seconds. One credit covers up to 10 seconds.
- Sign in with Google. The site is available in English, Korean, Japanese, Thai, and Vietnamese.
- The homepage can prefill a source link from trending TikTok examples.

## Current input and output notes

Supported source links:

- A public TikTok video link (a profile link is not a video)
- A public Instagram Reel link
- A YouTube Shorts link in the form `youtube.com/shorts/...`

Regular YouTube watch links and `youtu.be` links are not accepted.

The download is an MP4. Check the live product UI for the current plan gates, credit allowances, and export options.

## Privacy and use notes

Photos and linked videos are processed to generate the video you request and to run the account features around that request. SwapReels does not sell personal data or creative assets, does not use them for advertising profiles, does not claim ownership of your creative assets, and does not store payment card details. Your generation history is not public.

Use only photos, likenesses, and source clips you have permission to use. Misleading impersonation is prohibited. The [Privacy Policy](https://swapreels.com/privacy) and [Terms of Service](https://swapreels.com/terms) are the legal sources.

Public plans are Starter, Pro, Max, and Ultra. Subscribers with an active plan can buy extra credits. Prices, monthly allowances, and commercial-use rules belong on the live Pricing control and in the current Terms. Confirm them there before you publish client or brand work.
