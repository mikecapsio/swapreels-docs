# SwapReels vs Vidnoz

SwapReels and Vidnoz both sit in AI video, and they are built around different jobs.

SwapReels takes a public TikTok, Instagram Reel, or YouTube Short plus a photo, and returns that clip with a different person and the original audio. Vidnoz is an online AI video product with face swap among other generators: talking presenters, templates, and scenes that are not already a short.

Check Vidnoz's current catalog on Vidnoz's own site. This page stays inside what SwapReels publishes about the comparison.

## Short answer

Use Vidnoz when you want a wider video suite and the footage does not exist yet.

Use SwapReels when the video already exists as a public short and the change is who appears in it.

## SwapReels

- Paste a public short-form link. No prompt for the scene.
- Upload one photo.
- Keep the source hook, motion, and audio.
- Person swap for full-body clips as well as closer shots.
- 5 to 30 seconds, MP4 download.
- Paid plans: HD, no watermark.
- Credits: one credit covers up to 10 seconds.
- Plans named on the site: Starter, Pro, Max, and Ultra. Prices are in the live Pricing control.

## Vidnoz

- A broader online AI video product
- Face swap is one capability among other generators
- A fit for presenters, templates, or scenes you still need to create
- A slower path to a trend if you generate a new scene and then try to match a sound that already exists on a public short

SwapReels does not quote Vidnoz prices or data policies. Read those on Vidnoz's site.

## Choosing by the brief

| Brief | Start with |
| --- | --- |
| Put my photo in this public Reel and keep the sound | SwapReels |
| Make a presenter or a scene that does not exist | A suite such as Vidnoz |
| I already downloaded the clip and only have the file | An upload-first face swap such as Deepswap |
| I need cuts and captions | An editor, using the MP4 after a swap |

## Moving a short-form job to SwapReels

1. Find the public TikTok, Reel, or Short that already has the performance.
2. Sign in at https://swapreels.com.
3. Upload a clear photo that matches the framing.
4. Paste the link. For YouTube, use `youtube.com/shorts/...`.
5. Generate, preview, and download.
6. Confirm you can use the likeness and the source.

## Privacy

Photos and linked videos on SwapReels are processed for the generation you request. SwapReels does not sell them, does not build advertising profiles from them, and does not claim ownership of your creative assets. https://swapreels.com/privacy

## Bottom line

Vidnoz answers: make AI video, including with a wider set of generators.

SwapReels answers: change the person in this public short and leave the audio in place.
