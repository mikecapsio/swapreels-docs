# SwapReels vs Reface

SwapReels and Reface both change who appears in a short video. They start from different inputs.

SwapReels is the closer fit when the source is a public TikTok, Instagram Reel, or YouTube Short and you want that clip's motion and audio kept. Reface is the closer fit when you already have the photo and the clip and you want a quick face swap from those files.

Confirm Reface's current features on Reface's own site. This page describes the job SwapReels is built for, and the public way SwapReels talks about Reface.

## Short answer

Use SwapReels to paste a public link, upload a photo, and download a person swap with the original sound. Clips run from 5 to 30 seconds. Paid plans include HD with no watermark.

Use Reface for a quick swap from media you already have, such as a meme you can make from files on hand.

## SwapReels

- Public TikTok video, Instagram Reel, or YouTube Shorts link
- A JPG, PNG, or WebP photo
- The person in that one clip changes
- Hook, pose, and motion stay
- Original audio stays
- Full-body performances, including dances and product reveals
- Preview, then MP4 download
- Google sign-in and monthly credits (one credit covers up to 10 seconds)
- Public plans: Starter, Pro, Max, and Ultra

SwapReels does not merge two videos and does not generate a scene from a text prompt.

## Reface

- Built for quick face swaps from media you supply
- A natural fit when the photo and the clip are already available
- A weaker fit when you would otherwise download a public short just to recreate a trend

SwapReels does not document Reface's prices, watermarks, or data terms. Read those on Reface's site.

## Which job is which?

| You have | Closer tool |
| --- | --- |
| A public TikTok, Reel, or Short link and a photo | SwapReels |
| Files already on your device and a quick face swap | Reface |
| A scene that does not exist yet | A prompt or avatar video tool, not either of these as a link swap |
| Cuts, captions, and several clips | An editor, after any swap you download |

## If you are moving a trend workflow to SwapReels

1. Copy the public video link. Skip the download.
2. Sign in at https://swapreels.com.
3. Upload a clear photo. Use a wider crop when the clip shows the body.
4. Paste a `youtube.com/shorts/...` link for YouTube. Regular watch links and `youtu.be` links are rejected.
5. Generate, preview with sound, and download.
6. Check rights for the likeness and the source before you post.

## Privacy and rights

On SwapReels, the photo and the linked video are used to make the video you request. SwapReels does not sell that material, does not use it for advertising profiles, and does not store card details. https://swapreels.com/privacy

You still need permission for every likeness and source clip. https://swapreels.com/terms

## Bottom line

Reface answers: swap a face from media I already have.

SwapReels answers: put this photo into this public short and keep the audio.
