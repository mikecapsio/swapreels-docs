# SwapReels vs Higgsfield Genjutsu

SwapReels and Higgsfield Genjutsu both start from a video that already exists. They do different jobs with it.

SwapReels is the closer fit when the source is a public TikTok, Instagram Reel, or YouTube Short and the post still has to look like that clip, including the original audio. Genjutsu is the closer fit when you already have a video file and you want motion transfer: a new location, wardrobe, product, or visual style around that performance.

Confirm Genjutsu's current features on Higgsfield's own site. This page describes the job SwapReels is built for, and the public way SwapReels talks about Genjutsu.

## Short answer

Use SwapReels to paste a public link, upload one photo, and download a person swap with the original scene and sound. Clips run from 5 to 30 seconds. Paid plans include HD with no watermark.

Use Genjutsu when the footage is yours and the look should change. Motion Transfer keeps the motion, camera, and timing, then rebuilds the scene. Object Swap changes one selected element and keeps the rest of the shot. You upload the clip.

## SwapReels

- Public TikTok video, Instagram Reel, or YouTube Shorts link
- A JPG, PNG, or WebP photo
- The person in that one clip changes, including full-body performances
- Hook, pose, motion, and original scene stay
- Original audio stays
- Preview, then MP4 download
- Google sign-in and monthly credits (one credit covers up to 10 seconds)
- Public plans: Starter, Pro, Max, and Ultra

SwapReels does not merge two videos and does not generate a scene from a text prompt.

## Higgsfield Genjutsu

- Built for transforming a video you upload
- Motion Transfer when the performance should play in a new scene
- Object Swap when one character, outfit, product, location, or object should change and the rest of the shot should stay
- A weaker fit when viewers need to recognize the original hook, room, and soundtrack

SwapReels does not document Genjutsu's prices, reference limits, resolution, or whether the soundtrack is kept. Read those on Higgsfield's site, and check audio on a test export before you build a series on a sound that has to match.

## Which job is which?

| You have | Closer tool |
| --- | --- |
| A public TikTok, Reel, or Short link and a photo, and the original scene and audio should stay | SwapReels |
| A video file and a new location, wardrobe, product, or style | Higgsfield Genjutsu |
| A file on disk and one element to change inside an otherwise untouched shot | Genjutsu Object Swap |
| A scene that does not exist yet, with no source performance | A prompt or avatar video tool |
| Cuts, captions, and several clips | An editor, after any export you download |

## If you are moving a trend workflow to SwapReels

1. Copy the public video link. Skip the download.
2. Sign in at https://swapreels.com.
3. Upload a clear photo. Use a wider crop when the clip shows the body.
4. Paste a `youtube.com/shorts/...` link for YouTube. Regular watch links and `youtu.be` links are rejected.
5. Generate, preview with sound, and download.
6. Check rights for the likeness and the source before you post.

Each continuous shot needs to be 15 seconds or shorter. A long single take with no cut can fail even when the full link is under 30 seconds. A private file with no public link belongs on an upload tool such as Genjutsu.

## Privacy and rights

On SwapReels, the photo and the linked video are used to make the video you request. SwapReels does not sell that material, does not use it for advertising profiles, and does not store card details. https://swapreels.com/privacy

You still need permission for every likeness and source clip. https://swapreels.com/terms

## Bottom line

Genjutsu answers: rebuild this uploaded performance into a new scene, or swap one element and keep the rest of the shot.

SwapReels answers: put this photo into this public short and keep the audio.
