# SwapReels vs Deepswap

SwapReels and Deepswap both replace a person in a video with a face or photo you provide. The handoff is different.

SwapReels starts from a public TikTok, Instagram Reel, or YouTube Short link. Deepswap is an online face swap that starts from a face and a video you upload. Remaker and similar sites sit with Deepswap: bring the files, then export.

Confirm Deepswap's current product on Deepswap's own site before you rely on a feature list.

## Short answer

Use SwapReels when the clip is already a public short and you want the original motion and audio, without downloading it into an uploader first.

Use Deepswap when the video file is already on your computer.

## SwapReels

- One public link: TikTok video, Instagram Reel, or YouTube Shorts (`youtube.com/shorts/...`)
- One photo: JPG, PNG, or WebP
- Replaces the person in that single clip
- Keeps the hook, motion, and original audio
- 5 to 30 seconds
- Preview and MP4 download
- HD, no watermark on paid plans
- One credit covers up to 10 seconds
- Does not merge two clips

## Deepswap

- An online face swap for a face and a video you supply
- A direct fit when the source file is local
- An extra step when the source is a public short you would have to download first

SwapReels does not publish Deepswap's limits, pricing, or privacy terms. Those belong on Deepswap's site. The same applies to Remaker and other upload-first tools: compare them on watermark, length, and how they treat the photo you upload.

## When the source is public

1. Copy the TikTok, Reel, or Shorts URL.
2. Open https://swapreels.com and sign in with Google.
3. Upload the photo and paste the link.
4. Generate and preview.
5. Download the MP4. Use a paid plan for HD with no watermark.

If the only copy is a private file, and there is no public link, stay with an upload-first tool.

## What "person swap" means here

SwapReels replaces who is in the frame and follows the source performance. That includes full-body clips. A face-only pass is a better description of close-up swaps. Deepswap's public positioning, as SwapReels describes it, is the upload-a-face-and-a-video job.

## Privacy and rights

SwapReels uses your photo and the linked video to generate the requested download. It does not sell personal or creative data, does not use them for advertising profiles, and does not store payment card numbers. https://swapreels.com/privacy

Rights to the likeness and the source are still required. A public URL is not a license. https://swapreels.com/terms

## Bottom line

Deepswap answers: here is a face and a video file.

SwapReels answers: here is a photo and a public short link. Keep the sound.
