# SwapReels vs Akool

SwapReels and Akool are both used around AI video for content and marketing. The scope is different.

SwapReels is a single workflow: a public TikTok, Instagram Reel, or YouTube Short, a photo, a person swap, the original audio, a download. Akool is an AI video platform aimed at marketing teams, for campaigns that need a larger toolkit than one swapped short.

Check Akool's current tools on Akool's own site. This comparison uses the public description on SwapReels.

## Short answer

Use Akool when the campaign needs a broad AI video platform.

Use SwapReels when the brief is one public link and one photo.

## SwapReels

- Built for short-form person swap
- Inputs are a photo and a public link, not a production suite
- Keeps motion, hook, and original audio
- 5 to 30 seconds
- Preview and MP4
- HD with no watermark on paid plans
- Monthly credits on Starter, Pro, Max, and Ultra
- One credit covers up to 10 seconds
- Useful for UGC-style hooks, trend posts, and persona clips you are allowed to publish

## Akool

- An AI video platform for marketing teams
- A fit when one swapped short is not the whole job
- More tool than a creator needs for a single trend post

SwapReels does not list Akool's modules, prices, or contracts. Use Akool's site and your own vendor review for that.

## For UGC and social teams

A common SwapReels brief:

1. A public hook you have rights to use.
2. An approved photo of who should appear.
3. A download that still has the opener and the sound.
4. A paid HD file with no watermark for a brand or client.

That path does not include a timeline, a presenter studio, or a multi-tool campaign workspace. If the work needs those, Akool's category is the one to evaluate. If the work is "same hook, different person," SwapReels is the smaller product.

See [SwapReels for UGC creators](../for/ugc-creators.md).

## Switching a link-and-photo job

1. Collect the public TikTok, Reel, or Shorts URL.
2. Sign in at https://swapreels.com.
3. Upload the approved photo.
4. Generate and review the first second with sound.
5. Download and file the MP4 with the campaign assets.
6. Keep permission records for the likeness and the source. https://swapreels.com/terms

## Privacy

SwapReels uses uploads to deliver the video you request. It does not sell personal or creative data, does not use them for advertising profiles, and does not store card details. Generation history is not public. https://swapreels.com/privacy

## Bottom line

Akool answers: give a marketing team a broad AI video platform.

SwapReels answers: put this photo in this public short and keep the audio.
