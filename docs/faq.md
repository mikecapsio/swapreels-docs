# SwapReels FAQ

Short answers for the public product. The live FAQ at https://swapreels.com/faq is authoritative if a page there is newer than this file. Prices and monthly credit allowances are only in Pricing on https://swapreels.com.

## Product basics

### What is SwapReels?

SwapReels is an AI video character swap for public TikTok videos, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts. Paste a link, upload a photo, and the person in the clip changes while the motion and audio stay. Source clips can be up to 30 seconds.

### How does SwapReels work?

1. Sign in with Google at https://swapreels.com.
2. Upload a clear photo (JPG, PNG, or WebP).
3. Paste one public TikTok, Instagram Reel, or YouTube Shorts link.
4. Generate. This usually takes a few minutes.
5. Preview the video and download the MP4.

Paid plans include HD downloads with no watermark.

### What is the difference between face swap and person swap?

Face swap usually means replacing a face, which fits a close-up. SwapReels replaces the person in the clip and keeps the hook, motion, pose, and sound. That is the useful version when a dance, outfit, or product reveal uses the whole body.

https://swapreels.com/faq/what-is-ai-face-swap

### Who uses SwapReels?

People who already know which short they want to appear in: trend creators, dance pages, UGC marketers, social teams, brands, and persona or AI influencer accounts that reuse one approved photo.

### Does SwapReels write a new video from a prompt?

No. There is no prompt box for the scene. The inputs are a photo and a public short-form link.

### Can I merge two videos?

No. SwapReels replaces the person in one source video with someone from your photo. Paste one URL.

https://swapreels.com/faq/copy-person-face

## Links, photos, and length

### Which links are supported?

- Public TikTok video links. A profile link is not a video.
- Public Instagram Reel links.
- YouTube Shorts links in the form `youtube.com/shorts/...`.

Regular YouTube videos and `youtu.be` links are rejected. The clip must be public and available.

### How long can the source video be?

At least 5 seconds and at most 30 seconds. Longer trends need a shorter public cut before you paste the link.

https://swapreels.com/faq/video-length

### What photo should I upload?

JPG, PNG, or WebP. Use a clear, well-lit, front-facing photo of one person. Show the upper body or full body when the source clip is full-body. Sunglasses, blur, heavy filters, and tiny faces in a crowd weaken the result.

### Do I need to download the TikTok or Reel first?

No. Copy the public link and paste it in.

https://swapreels.com/faq/copy-tiktok-instagram

### What do I download?

An MP4. Preview it in the browser first. Completed videos can be downloaded again from account swap history. Paid plans include HD files with no watermark.

## Generation and quality

### How do I put myself in a TikTok, Reel, or Short?

Upload a clear photo, paste a public link up to 30 seconds, and generate. The result follows the original movement and audio, including the hook or choreography when that is what the clip is for.

https://swapreels.com/faq/put-yourself-in-video

### Can I copy a dance?

Yes, if you have the right to use the trend and the likeness. Paste the dance URL and upload your photo. You do not film the routine.

https://swapreels.com/faq/copy-dance-trend

### How do I start from a trending clip?

Pick a public short that is already working: a UGC hook, reaction, product reveal, dance, or recognizable format. Paste that link, swap in your photo, and post with the caption and hashtags the platform expects. The original sound stays, so the result can still sit in the trend.

https://swapreels.com/faq/viral-trending-face-swap

### Why do some results look wrong?

AI output varies. Weak inputs are the usual cause: a hidden face, bad light, several people crossing the frame, fast cuts, or a photo that does not match a full-body performance. Review every video before you publish.

### How long does generation take?

Usually a few minutes. If the page times out, check swap history. The job may still complete.

## Audio, watermarks, and credits

### Does the original audio stay?

Yes. Music and voice from the clip you linked stay in the finished video.

https://swapreels.com/faq/original-audio

### Is there a watermark?

Paid plans include HD downloads with no watermark. A free try is for checking the result. Use a paid plan when you need a clean file to post.

https://swapreels.com/faq/watermark-and-hd

### How do credits work?

One credit covers up to 10 seconds of source video. A 30-second clip uses three credits. Public plans are Starter, Pro, Max, and Ultra, each with a monthly allowance. Subscribers with an active plan can buy extra credits. Open Pricing on the website for current prices and allowances.

### How do I cancel?

Open **Manage subscription** in your account. You can cancel anytime. The plan stays active until the end of the current billing period.

## UGC, influencers, and posting

### Can I copy a UGC hook?

Paste a public TikTok, Reel, or Short you have rights to use, upload a reference photo, and generate. The person changes. The hook, motion, and audio stay. You still need permission for the likeness and the source.

https://swapreels.com/faq/copy-ugc-video-or-hook

### Can I make AI influencer-style videos?

You can put one consistent photo into many public shorts. Use your own likeness, a look you have rights to, or a character you own. Misleading impersonation is prohibited under the Terms.

https://swapreels.com/faq/ai-influencer-videos

### Where can I post the download?

Wherever you have rights to post it, including TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts. Platform rules for music, disclosure, and synthetic media are separate from SwapReels.
