# SwapReels Getting Started Guide

SwapReels puts a person from your photo into a public TikTok, Instagram Reel, or YouTube Short. The moves, hook, and original audio stay. You download an MP4.

The basic workflow is:

1. Sign in and make sure the account has credits.
2. Choose a public short you have the right to use.
3. Upload a clear photo.
4. Generate, preview, and download.
5. Review the clip before you post.

## Before you start

Prepare:

- A Google account for sign-in
- A SwapReels plan with credits, if you need an HD file with no watermark
- A public TikTok video, Instagram Reel, or YouTube Shorts link
- A clear photo of a person you have permission to show
- A place to post, if the result is for TikTok, Reels, Shorts, or a client

Source clips must be at least 5 seconds and at most 30 seconds. One credit covers up to 10 seconds, so a full 30-second clip uses three credits. Open Pricing on https://swapreels.com for the current monthly allowance.

## Step 1: Open SwapReels and sign in

Go to https://swapreels.com. English uses that URL. Korean, Japanese, Thai, and Vietnamese use `/ko/`, `/ja/`, `/th/`, and `/vi/`.

Click **Sign in** and complete **Sign in with Google**. An account is required before a video can be generated.

If sign-in stops, try again in a browser that allows the Google prompt. The site may say that Google sign-in did not complete.

## Step 2: Check credits

Open **Pricing** in the header, or use **Choose plan** on the generator if it appears. Public plans are Starter, Pro, Max, and Ultra. Continuing through checkout means you agree to the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

Paid plans include HD downloads with no watermark. A free try is for checking the result. Use a paid plan when you need a clean file to post.

Subscribers with an active plan can buy extra credits from the account flow when a clip needs more than the remaining allowance. Prices and package sizes are shown only in the live UI.

You can cancel later from **Manage subscription**. The plan stays active until the end of the current billing period.

## Step 3: Pick the source clip

Copy a public link. SwapReels reads that URL. You do not download the file into an editor first.

Use:

- A TikTok video link, not a profile link
- An Instagram Reel link
- A YouTube Shorts link shaped like `https://www.youtube.com/shorts/...`

Leave out regular YouTube watch links and `youtu.be` links.

Prefer a clip with:

- One main person
- A face and body you can actually see
- Steady framing and usable light
- A hook, dance, or gesture that is easy to follow
- A length between 5 and 30 seconds

Group dances, fast cuts, heavy blur, and strobe lighting give the swap less stable motion to follow.

The homepage may show trending TikTok cards. **Use this video** fills the source field. Those cards are examples. You still need rights for anything you generate, and you can always paste your own link.

## Step 4: Upload the photo

Use JPG, PNG, or WebP. A clear, well-lit, front-facing photo works best.

Match the photo to the clip:

- A close-up source can work from a clear head-and-shoulders photo.
- A dance, outfit, or product reveal works better when the photo shows the upper body or full body.
- Skip sunglasses, heavy filters, motion blur, and crowded shots where the person is tiny.

The photo panel may show **Optimizing image...** while the file is prepared. Replace the photo if the preview looks wrong.

## Step 5: Generate

Click **Swap**. The button moves through progress states such as preparing and generating. A generation usually takes a few minutes. Leave the page open until the status shows the video is ready.

If the account has no active allowance, the control asks you to choose a plan instead of starting.

Each successful generation uses credits from the current allowance. The account area shows how many credits are available and how many generations are in progress.

## Step 6: Preview and download

Open **Preview**. Play the video and check:

- Whether the person reads as the photo you uploaded
- Whether the hook and motion still make sense
- Whether hands, edges, or lighting look broken
- Whether the original sound is the sound you expected

Download the MP4 from the preview, or later from **Account → Swap history** on items marked ready. Paid downloads are HD and have no watermark.

The file is yours to post where you have rights: TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, or elsewhere.

## Step 7: Use the account

**Account** shows:

- The name and email from Google sign-in
- Plan status, such as an active monthly plan, payment processing, cancellation at period end, or no active plan
- Available credits and in-progress generations
- Swap history with download links for completed videos

**Manage subscription** opens billing management for payment method, invoices, or cancellation. SwapReels does not store card numbers.

## Step 8: Post with the rights you actually have

Before a clip goes on a public account:

- Confirm you can use the source video and its audio.
- Confirm you can use the likeness in the photo.
- Reject results that impersonate someone in a misleading or harmful way.
- Add context or disclosure when the platform or the audience needs it.
- Skip private moments, unclear permissions, and clips that would read as a fake endorsement.

The Terms of Service cover acceptable use. Support is team@swapreels.com.

## Common problems

### The generator asks you to choose a plan

Sign in, then subscribe or wait until payment processing finishes. Check **Account** for the current status and remaining credits.

### The photo is rejected

Use JPG, PNG, or WebP, with one clearly visible person. If optimization fails, try another file.

### The link is rejected

Paste a full public TikTok video, Instagram Reel, or `youtube.com/shorts/...` URL. Profile links, regular YouTube videos, and `youtu.be` links do not work. Private, removed, or unavailable videos can fail even when the URL looks right.

### The clip is the wrong length

Stay between 5 and 30 seconds. Trim a longer short on the original platform, then paste the new public link.

### Generation is busy or fails

Wait and try again, or use a clearer photo and a simpler source clip. If the browser stops waiting, refresh and check swap history. The job may still finish.

### The download is missing

Use **Preview** after the video is ready, or **Account → Swap history → Download**.
