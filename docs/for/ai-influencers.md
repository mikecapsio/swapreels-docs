# SwapReels for AI Influencers

SwapReels is an AI video character swap for persona-led short-form accounts. Reuse one approved photo across public TikTok videos, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts. Each download keeps that clip's motion and audio, with your look in the frame.

The account still needs a point of view. A random stack of swaps does not read as a creator.

## What you get

- One reference look placed into many existing formats
- Original hook, motion, and sound from each source link
- Person swap for dances, lifestyle clips, reactions, and UGC-style scenes
- MP4 downloads, HD and watermark-free on paid plans
- A 5 to 30 second limit per source
- Credits you can spend across a series: one credit covers up to 10 seconds

SwapReels does not design the persona, write the captions, or generate a scene from a character description. You bring the photo and the links.

## Build the persona before the batch

Decide the look and the niche first.

- Use one approved reference: your likeness, a model with permission, or a character asset you own.
- Pick a small set of content pillars, such as fashion, fitness, comedy, or product-adjacent UGC.
- Choose source clips that match that niche.
- Drop outputs where the face no longer matches the rest of the feed.

| A stronger use | A weaker use | A safer path |
| --- | --- | --- |
| An owned persona across trends | A real person's face without consent | Your likeness, a licensed look, or a character you own |
| Trend posts labeled so the audience understands them | A clip presented as a real event that did not happen | Creative framing and disclosure when it is needed |
| A fictional ambassador with clear rights | A celebrity or brand impersonation | A distinct character, not a copy of a famous person |

Misleading impersonation is prohibited. https://swapreels.com/terms

## Repeatable workflow

1. Save public links that fit the persona. Prefer one person, clear light, and a format you would actually post.
2. Sign in at https://swapreels.com and upload the same reference photo each time, unless you are deliberately changing the look.
3. Generate one candidate at a time until the prompt-free inputs are dialed in: photo quality and source cleanliness.
4. Preview with sound. Reject broken motion and identity drift.
5. Download on a paid plan when the file needs to be HD with no watermark.
6. Post with the caption, cover, and disclosure that account uses.
7. Keep the links that worked. Reuse those formats.

A 30-second source uses three credits. Check Pricing before a large series. Public plans are Starter, Pro, Max, and Ultra.

## Photo standard

The same photo is the identity system. Use a clear, front-facing JPG, PNG, or WebP. Match the crop to the clips: full-body sources need a photo that shows the body. Changing the photo every post makes the account look like a demo reel.

## Privacy

The photo and the linked videos are processed to create the downloads you ask for. SwapReels does not sell that material or use it for advertising profiles. Your history stays on your account. https://swapreels.com/privacy

## Start here

1. Lock one reference photo you have rights to use.
2. Pick three public shorts in the same niche.
3. Generate, keep the consistent results, and post those.
