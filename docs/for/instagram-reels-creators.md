# SwapReels for Instagram Reels Creators

SwapReels is an AI video character swap for Instagram Reels. Paste a public Reel link, upload a photo, and download a short that keeps that Reel's motion and audio, with a different person in the frame.

An "AI Reel maker" can mean a prompt-to-video tool, a template editor, or a caption app. SwapReels is the narrower job: an existing public Reel, a photo, a person swap, a download.

## What you get

- A Reel-length workflow from a public link, up to 30 seconds
- Original audio kept in the file
- Person swap for clips where the body, outfit, or gesture matters
- Preview, then an MP4
- HD with no watermark on paid plans
- The same generator for TikTok and YouTube Shorts when you repurpose a format across apps

You still choose the cover frame, caption, and whether the clip belongs on your grid.

## What makes a Reel a good source

Reels reward a clear first second. Prefer:

- One main person
- Movement you can follow
- A simple hook over a chaotic edit
- Public clips you have permission to reuse

Skip private stories, sensitive footage, and Reels where several people cross the frame. Instagram feed posts and profile URLs are not Reel links. Paste a `/reel/` or `/reels/` URL.

## Reel workflow

### 1. Separate the search from the tool

| If you are trying to | Use |
| --- | --- |
| Make a Reel faster, from an idea | A prompt, template, or editor workflow, then judge it on its own |
| Replace who appears in a Reel that already exists | SwapReels: link plus photo |
| Reuse a format or trend | A public Reel you have rights to, then a person swap |

### 2. Generate

Sign in at https://swapreels.com with Google. Upload JPG, PNG, or WebP. Paste the Reel link. Click **Swap**.

One credit covers up to 10 seconds. A 30-second Reel uses three credits. Open Pricing for the current plan allowance. Public plans are Starter, Pro, Max, and Ultra.

### 3. Review for the grid

Watch the preview before you download. A Reel can look fine in motion and still fail as a cover: an awkward first frame, a face that does not match your other posts, or a sound that fights the caption you plan to write.

Paid plans include HD downloads with no watermark, which matters if the Reel is for a brand or a client.

### 4. Publish on Instagram's terms

Post the MP4 as a Reel with the caption and disclosure your account needs. SwapReels changes who is in the video. It does not write the caption or clear music rights beyond keeping the source audio in the file. You confirm those rights.

If you also post to TikTok or Shorts, the same download can go there when you have permission. The generator accepts those links too.

## Photo notes for Reels

A talking-head Reel can start from a clear head-and-shoulders photo. A try-on, dance, or full-body hook needs a photo that shows the body. Front light and a single person beat a filtered group shot.

## Rights and fit

Use likenesses and Reels you are allowed to use. Do not imply a person endorsed a product if they did not. The Terms prohibit misleading impersonation: https://swapreels.com/terms

Choose a broader AI video suite when you need a scene that is not already a Reel. Choose an editor when the Reel needs cuts and stickers after the swap. See [Vidnoz](../vs/vidnoz.md) and [Akool](../vs/akool.md).
