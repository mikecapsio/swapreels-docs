# SwapReels for AI Creators

SwapReels is an AI video character swap for people who publish short-form video with AI in the workflow. Paste a public TikTok, Instagram Reel, or YouTube Short, upload a photo, and download a clip that keeps the motion and audio.

AI creator work on SwapReels is still a link-and-photo job. It covers trend posts, UGC-style ads, and persona series. It does not cover prompt-to-video scenes, long-form edits, or avatar studios.

## What you get

- A public short as the motion and audio source
- Your person, from a photo, in that clip
- A preview and an MP4
- HD with no watermark on paid plans
- Source clips from 5 to 30 seconds
- One credit per 10 seconds

You can run the same steps for a dance, a meme, a reaction, or a creator-style ad. The generator does not change.

## Three jobs, one workflow

| Goal | Source to paste | What to keep |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Trend post | A dance, reaction, meme, or POV that is already circulating | Original audio and a recognizable first second |
| UGC-style ad | A public hook with one person and a clear offer structure | Product visibility and the spoken or gestured opener |
| Persona series | Clean creator-style Reels or TikToks in one niche | The same reference photo across posts |

Steps:

1. Sign in with Google at https://swapreels.com.
2. Upload JPG, PNG, or WebP. One clear person. Match the crop to the clip.
3. Paste one public link. TikTok video, Instagram Reel, or `youtube.com/shorts/...`.
4. Generate and preview. A run usually takes a few minutes.
5. Download. Use a paid plan when the file must be HD and watermark-free.
6. Add the caption, cover, and any disclosure outside SwapReels.

## How this differs from other AI video tools

Prompt and avatar tools create footage that did not exist. Editors assemble a timeline. Upload-first face swaps start from a file on your computer. SwapReels starts from a public short that already has a performance.

If your week is "appear in formats that are already working," this is the direct path. If your week is "invent a scene," use a generator built for that, then come back here only when you have a real short to swap.

## Publishing rules

Use likenesses and source clips you have rights to. Do not publish a video that deceives people about who appeared or who endorsed a product. Disclose AI-assisted content when the platform or your audience expects it.

The Terms prohibit misleading impersonation and using a likeness without permission: https://swapreels.com/terms

Uploads are used to make the requested video. They are not sold and are not used for advertising profiles: https://swapreels.com/privacy

## Credits

Public plans are Starter, Pro, Max, and Ultra. One credit covers up to 10 seconds, so plan a 30-second source as three credits. Active subscribers can buy extra credits. Amounts and prices are in the live Pricing control.
