# SwapReels for TikTok Creators

SwapReels is an AI video character swap for TikTok. Paste a public TikTok link, upload a photo, and download a short where that person follows the original hook and motion. The original audio stays.

It is for creators who already know which TikTok they want to appear in: a dance, a UGC hook, a reaction, a meme, a POV, or a product reveal.

## What you get

- A finished short from a public link and a photo
- The source movement, including choreography when that is the point of the clip
- The original sound, so the post can still match the trend
- A person swap, which holds up better than a face-only crop when the body is in frame
- An MP4 preview and download
- HD with no watermark on paid plans
- Clips from 5 to 30 seconds
- One credit per 10 seconds of source video

SwapReels does not write a TikTok from a caption prompt, and it does not edit a timeline. You still write the caption, choose the cover, and decide whether the result is good enough to post.

## A TikTok workflow

### 1. Save the link while the trend is usable

Copy the public video URL from TikTok. A profile link is not enough. If the clip is longer than 30 seconds, use a public cut that fits the limit. Clips under 5 seconds are too short.

Look for one visible person, a readable first second, and motion you can follow. Fast cuts, crowds, and heavy blur are harder sources.

### 2. Match the photo to the clip

Upload JPG, PNG, or WebP. A clear, front-facing photo is the baseline. If the TikTok is a full-body dance or a product-in-hand reveal, use a photo that shows more than a tight face crop.

Use a likeness you have permission to show. Your own photo is the straightforward case.

### 3. Generate and watch the first second

Sign in at https://swapreels.com, paste the link, and click **Swap**. Generation usually takes a few minutes.

Play the preview with sound. Check identity, hands, edges, and whether the hook still lands. Download from the preview or from account swap history.

The homepage can also offer trending TikTok cards. **Use this video** only fills the link. It does not clear your responsibility for rights.

### 4. Post like a TikTok, not like an export

The download is the visual. The post still needs a native caption, hashtags if you use them, and a cover that reads on the grid. If the sound is why the trend works, leave that audio in place. SwapReels already keeps it.

Review before you publish. Look for broken motion, a face that drifted, or a result that would mislead someone about who was in the original moment. Add context when the joke or the AI assistance needs it.

## Formats that tend to work

| Format | What to look for in the source | What to check in the result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dance | One dancer, steady frame, visible body | The routine still reads, and the photo matches a full-body clip |
| UGC hook | A clear opener in the first second | The hook timing and voice stayed |
| Reaction or meme | A recognizable setup | The joke is obvious without extra explanation |
| Product reveal | The product and the person are both readable | The swap did not hide the product |
| POV | One person, simple motion | The angle still makes sense for your page |

## When another tool fits better

Use an upload-first face swap when the TikTok file is already on your computer and there is no public link. Use a prompt-to-video tool when you need a scene that does not exist yet. Use an editor when you need cuts and on-screen text after the swap.

You need permission for the source clip, the audio, and the likeness. Misleading impersonation is prohibited. TikTok's own rules still apply when you post.
