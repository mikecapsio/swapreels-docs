# SwapReels for Dance Trend Creators

SwapReels is an AI video character swap for dance trends. Paste a public dance TikTok, Instagram Reel, or YouTube Short, upload a photo, and download a version where that person performs the same routine. The music stays.

You do not learn the choreography on camera first. The source clip supplies the moves. Your photo supplies who the viewer sees.

## What you get

- The original choreography and timing
- The original audio
- A full-person swap, which dance clips need more than a face overlay
- An MP4 from 5 to 30 seconds
- HD with no watermark on paid plans
- As many new dances as your credit allowance covers, each from its own public link

SwapReels does not invent a new dance from a description. If the routine is not in a public short you can link, this is the wrong tool.

## Pick a dance clip worth copying

Treat the source as the choreography. It should show the dance clearly.

Use:

- One dancer
- Full-body or upper-body framing when the moves leave the face
- Steady camera and enough light to see the body
- A public link you are allowed to reuse
- A length between 5 and 30 seconds

Avoid:

- Group routines where people cross
- Strobe, heavy blur, or a stack of jump cuts
- Clips that crop out the movement the trend is known for

YouTube sources must be Shorts URLs (`youtube.com/shorts/...`), not regular videos or `youtu.be` links.

## The photo matters more here

A face-only crop is a weak reference for a full-body dance. Use a clear, well-lit, front-facing photo that shows the upper body or the full body. Skip sunglasses, heavy filters, and tiny faces in a crowd.

JPG, PNG, and WebP are the supported types.

## Dance workflow

1. Copy the public dance link while the sound is still relevant to your page.
2. Sign in at https://swapreels.com.
3. Upload the photo and paste the link.
4. Generate. A run usually takes a few minutes. One credit covers up to 10 seconds.
5. Preview with sound. Watch the whole body, not only the face. Hands, feet, and turns are where weak sources show up.
6. Download the MP4 on a paid plan if you need HD without a watermark.
7. Post with a caption that fits the trend. If the remix could confuse people about who danced it, say so.

| Post | Source to look for | Caption job |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Join a rising dance | One dancer, clear routine, known sound | Ship while the sound still fits the feed |
| Funny remix | A recognizable routine and an unexpected but permitted likeness | Make the joke obvious |
| A series on one page | Routines that match the rest of the grid | Keep the look consistent |

## Quality bar

Discard a result when the body breaks, the face drifts mid-turn, or the routine is no longer recognizable. Generate again from a cleaner link or a clearer photo. Credits apply per generation. Check the live allowance before a large batch.

## Rights

Dance audio and choreography can be protected even when a link is public. You need the rights to reuse that clip and to show the person in the photo. The Terms prohibit deceptive impersonation.
