# SwapReels for UGC Creators

SwapReels is an AI video character swap for UGC-style shorts. Paste a public TikTok, Instagram Reel, or YouTube Short that already has the hook, upload a photo of who should appear, and download a clip that keeps the motion and audio.

User-generated content here means a short that feels like a person made it for the feed: a direct line, a simple setup, a product in hand, a reaction. The hook is the opening beat that earns the next second.

## What you get

- A creator-style short without filming that take
- The source hook, gesture, and timing
- The original audio, including a spoken opener when the source has one
- A person from your photo in that one clip
- An MP4, with HD and no watermark on paid plans
- A 5 to 30 second range that matches typical UGC hooks

SwapReels does not replace a shoot when the performance, product handling, or claim has to be captured for real. It replaces the step of re-filming a format that already exists in a public clip you are allowed to use.

## What you can copy

Useful public sources:

- Problem-then-payoff openers
- Product reveals and try-ons
- Reaction and POV setups
- Founder-style pitches and creator-style ads

The source still needs one visible person, readable motion, and a public link. A busy montage is a poor hook source.

## Production workflow

1. Pick a public TikTok, Reel, or Short with the UGC structure you want, between 5 and 30 seconds.
2. Confirm you can use that clip and its audio.
3. Upload a reference photo you have rights to show: your own, a creator you have permission for, or another approved look.
4. Paste the link at https://swapreels.com and generate.
5. Preview. Check that the product stays visible and the spoken hook still matches the picture.
6. Download. Paid plans include HD with no watermark, which is the file to hand a brand or a client.
7. Post, or pass the MP4 into an editor if you still need captions.

| You provide | The swap does | You still own |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A public short link | Replaces the person in that clip | Hook timing and motion |
| A reference photo | Places that look in the source | The original audio |
| Rights to both | Returns an MP4 | The decision to publish |

One credit covers up to 10 seconds. A 30-second hook uses three credits. Monthly allowances are in Pricing. Public plans are Starter, Pro, Max, and Ultra. Active subscribers can buy extra credits.

## Team habits that keep a batch usable

- Save links that match one offer, not every viral clip you see.
- Reuse the same approved photo when the series should feel like one creator.
- Reject outputs where the face drifts or the product claim no longer matches the frame.
- Keep a note of which formats you are allowed to reuse.

Volume helps only when each file is still lawful and on-brief.

## When to film anyway

Film when the product must be shown in your hands for the first time, when the line must be your recording, or when no public clip matches the offer. Use a marketing suite when the campaign needs many tools beyond one swapped short. See [Akool](../vs/akool.md).
